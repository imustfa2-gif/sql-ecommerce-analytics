# Key Business Findings

## Executive Performance

- Total orders: 99,441.
- Delivered orders: 96,478 (97.02%).
- Canceled orders: 625 (0.63%).
- Average payment value per order: approximately 160.99 BRL.

## Revenue and Products

- November 2017 generated the highest recorded monthly revenue in 2017.
- Health & Beauty generated the highest product sales value.
- High-performing products and categories reached strong sales through different combinations of volume and value per order.

## Customers and Geography

- Unique customers: 96,096.
- Repeat customers: 2,997 (3.12%).
- São Paulo generated the highest state-level revenue because of its large customer and order volume.

## Payments

- Credit cards generated 78.34% of all payment value.
- Higher-value credit-card purchases were generally associated with longer installment plans.
- Two zero-installment credit-card records were documented as data-quality exceptions.

## Delivery and Satisfaction

- Average delivery duration: 12.50 days.
- On-time delivery rate: 93.23%.
- Late delivery rate: 6.77%.
- Late orders were delayed by 10.62 days on average.
- On-time deliveries averaged 4.29 stars; late deliveries averaged 2.27 stars.

## Sellers and Freight

- Top sellers achieved high sales through different combinations of order volume and value per order.
- High sales did not always correspond with the highest customer ratings or strongest delivery performance.
- Freight cost equaled 16.57% of product sales value overall.
- Several states faced a freight burden above 20% of product value.

## Interpretation Note

These findings describe associations in the available dataset. They do not, by themselves, establish causation. Data limitations and exceptional records are documented in the SQL scripts and data-quality documentation.
